    Comment codes

    The operator comment codes as issued. Gaps in the numbering match the source sheet. A code is a field in the event log, so selecting 46 Burglary or 50 Suspicious Person is correct even though those exact words have no place in a report narrative. Pick the code, write the conduct.

      Camera observation terminology

      Twenty terms for video narratives, incident reports and CCTV review notes. The rule behind all of them: describe what the camera showed, never what you believe the subject intended.

        Words to keep out of a report

        Intent and mental state. Tried to, intended to, attempted to steal, was going to, decided to.

        Character and conclusion. Suspicious, nervous, shady, casing the store, loitering with intent, obviously, clearly.

        Legal conclusions. Stole, shoplifted, trespassed, assaulted. State the conduct and let the court name the crime.

        Reference language for report writing, not legal advice. Admissibility standards vary by jurisdiction and by what local prosecutors want to see. Run it past counsel before it goes into an SOP.
